% --- Baseline alignment group 1: simple → deep sub/superscripts ---

Plain math should sit on the baseline like this: $a$, $a+b$, $a=b+c$,
and with operators: $a \cdot b$, $a \times b$, $a \bmod b$.

Ascenders: $b, d, f, h, k, l, t$ vs descenders: $g, j, p, q, y$.  Put them
together: $b_j$, $h_p$, $f_q$, $k_y$ — the subscript hangs below baseline.

Deep subscripts: $a_b$, $a_{b_c}$, $a_{b_{c_d}}$, $a_{b_{c_{d_e}}}$.
Deep superscripts: $a^b$, $a^{b^c}$, $a^{b^{c^d}}$, $a^{b^{c^{d^e}}}$.
Both: $x^{y^z}_{i_j}$, $x_{i_j}^{y^z}$ — baseline should not drift.

% --- Group 2: nested fractions and roots ---

Inline fractions: $\tfrac{1}{2}$, $\tfrac{a}{b}$, and compare with display
$\frac{1}{2}$, $\frac{a+b}{c-d}$.

Nested fractions: $\frac{a}{\frac{b}{c}}$, $\frac{\frac{a}{b}}{\frac{c}{d}}$,
and the continued fraction $\frac{1}{1 + \frac{1}{1 + \frac{1}{1+x}}}$.

Roots: $\sqrt{x}$, $\sqrt[3]{x}$, $\sqrt{\sqrt{x}}$,
$\sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}$, $\sqrt[n]{\frac{a}{b}}$.

% --- Group 8: mixed inline in prose ---

The function $f: \R^n \to \R$ is \emph{continuous} at $x_0$ when
$\lim_{x \to x_0} f(x) = f(x_0)$.  Its derivative, if it exists, is
$f'(x_0) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x_0 + h) - f(x_0)}{h}$.  For smooth $f$,
the $n$-th order Taylor expansion reads
$f(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{f^{(k)}(x_0)}{k!}(x - x_0)^k + R_n(x)$.

% --- Group 9: functional analysis (ported from Typst real_world.typ) ---

Let $V$ be a Hilbert space over $\C$ with inner product $\inner{\cdot}{\cdot}$.
A bounded linear operator $T\colon V \to V$ satisfies
\[
\opnorm{T} = \sup_{\norm{x} \le 1} \norm{T x} < \infty.
\]
The spectrum of $T$ is
\[
\sigma(T) = \{\,\lambda \in \C : T - \lambda I \text{ is not invertible}\,\}.
\]
The spectral radius satisfies
\[
\rho(T) = \lim_{n \to \infty} \opnorm{T^n}^{1/n}.
\]

% --- Group 10: probability / information theory ---

Information entropy: $H(X) = -\sum_{x \in \mathcal{X}} p(x) \log p(x)$.

KL divergence: $\displaystyle D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P \,\|\, Q) = \sum_{x} p(x)
\log\frac{p(x)}{q(x)}$.

MLE: $\hat{\theta}_{\text{MLE}} = \argmax_{\theta \in \Theta} \prod_{i=1}^{n}
p(x_i \mid \theta)$.

Gaussian: $\mathcal{N}(x \mid \mu, \sigma^2) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2}}
\exp\!\left(-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right)$.
